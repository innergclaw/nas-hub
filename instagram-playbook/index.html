Do I have to pay for other tools?

No. It works on the free plans of Claude, Metricool and Instagram, using the regular Claude app on the web, desktop or your phone. Free Claude accounts get fewer messages a day, so if you post a lot you may want Pro. Claude Code needs Pro or Max, but this playbook doesn't use it. Metricool's free plan covers up to 20 scheduled posts a month, which is plenty for 3 to 5 posts a week. Plans change, so check each tool's pricing page.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can copy a share link and paste a message, you can do this. The setup is clicking through sign-ups and approving connections.

Do I need a Business account?

Yes, a professional account: Business or Creator. Instagram only lets scheduling tools post to those, and switching is free in the app. The playbook recommends Business, because Creator accounts can't auto-publish Stories.
